    How the Town Reads

    Your heroes are your bots. When a hero takes trades they walk out the south gate to camp by the fire. When a trade closes they come home: wins go to the vault and then the tavern, losses go home to sulk.
    Two researchers keep the heroes' watchlists fresh. Mistress Quill, the Loremaster, studies the busiest stocks, the biggest movers and the day's news every 30 minutes while the market is open. Corvin the Star-Reader watches every coin day and night from his star tower and reports every 15 minutes. Both pin their leads on the guild board. Pip finds his own penny stocks.
    Townsfolk live their own lives: merchants at the market, farmers in the fields, the smith at the anvil, guards on patrol, children in the square. At night most go home.
    Prosperity fills the town: more people, more houses, flower boxes, bunting and golden light. Losses bring a curse: empty lanes, ruins, rain, crows and fresh graves.
    Purses: each hero trades with a purse ($1,000 to start), split across several trades at once. Profit above the purse goes to the vault. Set aside half the purse 3 times in 7 days for a bigger purse.
    Day and night follow whether the US stock market is open. All amounts are paper money.
